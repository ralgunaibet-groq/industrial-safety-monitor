- **Coral Accent**: `#F26A4F` - Secondary brand color

- **Dark Background**: `#0A0A0F` - Deep, professional dark mode
- **Card Background**: `#1A1A2E` - Elevated surfaces

### Typography
- **Font Family**: Inter (modern, clean, professional)
- **Weight Range**: 400-800 for hierarchy
- **Letter Spacing**: Optimized for readability
- **Antialiasing**: Smooth rendering on all devices
